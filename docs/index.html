Controls

Move with arrows / WASD or the left stick. Jump with Space or A / Cross. Hold Down and tap Jump to charge a spindash. Pause with P / Escape or Start / Options. Use the pause menu to select a course, movement profile, or PHP Info.

The corner buttons control mute and fullscreen. Press F9 for diagnostics.
