# Build an editable landscape — drafting outline

I. Choose the ground
   A. Introduce the optional package, the sandbox, the reference chapter, and the API.
   B. Establish meters, bounds, lattice spacing, and lazy generation.
   C. Separate authoritative queries from visual detail.
   D. Name the presets and the recipe controls that shape and place a landform.
   E. Explain slope-guided amplitude attenuation in plain language.
   F. Bound and pin regions for physical bodies.
II. Join landscapes into a journey
   A. Lay out a departure map and its destinations.
   B. Show the join recipe.
   C. Explain shared sea level and roads across the layout.
III. Prepare an edit before publishing it
   A. Name the brush and road operations.
   B. Show candidate preparation and fixed-update publication.
   C. Explain transactions, road revisions, saves, and picking.
IV. Draw the scene
   A. Construct the scene and call update and render.
   B. Describe the nested grids and the look-ahead point.
   C. Mention charts, context restore, and close.
V. Shape the look of the surface
   A. Introduce the layer stack and show one added layer.
   B. Replace the appearance for broad changes.
   C. Introduce standing grass.
VI. Add weather, water, and sky
   A. Assign and edit weather.
   B. Use the same wave model for rendering and contacts.
   C. Connect sun controls, scattering, mist layers, and stars.
VII. Keep resources bounded
   A. Keep coarse coverage while preparing detail.
   B. Inspect counters and measure on the intended hardware.
   C. State heightfield and simulation limits.
