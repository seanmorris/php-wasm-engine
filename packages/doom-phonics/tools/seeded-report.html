Each layer begins with an embedded planar graph. Large anchor nodes establish the layout; smaller surrounding nodes receive decreasing area targets. Rooms combine one to three rectangles with opposing proportions, alongside faceted circular anchors. These footprints, shared walls and entrances conform to the graph. The report records cuts, merges, reserved stairs and clearance repairs. Ordinary stairs occupy wide enclosed connecting hallways; large rooms can have raised ledges with wrapping stairs recessed into their sides. Stairs have 256-unit top landings; passages retain 128 units of width and curved stairs retain 160. Fill the basement pool to reach the raised ledge, then climb the spiral to the upper floor. Recessed doors open with Use. Galleries and shafts provide downward shortcuts with return routes through the layer networks. Solid pillars and lowered centers occupy suitable rooms. The soulsphere marks the exploration goal.
