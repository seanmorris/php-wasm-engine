# Playing and controls

W/S moves, A/D strafes, the mouse or right stick looks, E or right click uses, and Space, left click, or RB fires. Number keys or the D-pad select weapons. Escape releases pointer lock and P or Start pauses.

The title menu starts single-player or multiplayer modes, opens the WAD stack, and exposes controls and look settings. Gameplay bindings are stored independently for keyboard/mouse and controller. Mouse displacement and stick axes feed the same named look actions after device-specific sensitivity and deadzone processing.

Episode starts, cooperative starts, and deathmatch starts are selected for the requested mode. Difficulty filters retain unflagged objects and include multiplayer-only objects only in multiplayer.

The status bar shows health, armor, ammo, keys, face state, arms, and the currently relevant ammo maximums. Pickups and cheats update the same typed inventory used by weapon firing.
